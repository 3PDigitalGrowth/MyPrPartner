## The Crisis Masterclass in one breath

A 12-month online crisis communications course from My PR Partner, powered by CRC Public Relations. It is built around the Crisis Ready® course by Melissa Agnes, founder and CEO of the Crisis Ready Institute in the USA, recorded for the Australasian program with Lyall Mercer. It is available in Australasia only through My PR Partner. The line on the page: "This course doesn't build plans, it builds you."

## Who it is for

PR and communications professionals (including PR agency teams), business and not-for-profit leaders and managers, media spokespeople, and marketing, HR and legal people who play a part when a crisis hits. Experience level doesn't matter: it suits people new to crisis work and seasoned practitioners.

## How it works

- 100% online and self-paced, available anywhere in Australasia.
- Enrolment runs for 12 months from the day you join. Everyone starts at month one the day they enrol, and a new session unlocks every 30 days. There is no cohort to keep up with.
- Each monthly session: a video session presented by Melissa Agnes, with Lyall Mercer's introduction and a closing conversation between the two of them on how the ideas play out in Australian organisations; a workbook (reflection prompts, frameworks and templates applied to the high-risk scenarios each member picks in month two; month three adds a full crisis communication exercise with a model example); and a downloadable resource.
- Monthly Q&A: members submit questions in advance and Melissa and Lyall work through them, usually against real situations members are dealing with. Recordings stay available for the rest of the enrolment.
- Fortnightly "Spotlight" emails between sessions with practical tips on reputation and crisis communication.
- Training resources from Lyall and other My PR Partner experts, plus bonus content.
- Time: most members set aside two to three hours a month.
- Anything new released during a member's 12 months (new Australian case studies, new Crisis Ready® content) is included at no extra cost.

## The twelve months

Released sessions:
1. Defining crisis readiness: mindset before methodology; the Crisis Response Penalty; connection as the goal; core values as decision anchors.
2. Issue vs crisis: the Crisis vs Issue framework, the five categories of material impact, setting your thresholds, choosing your high-risk scenarios.
3. You can't beat emotion with logic: the Validate, Relate, Prove formula (prove with reassurance, hope and courage); the science of connection.
4. Four levels of safety (physical, psychological, emotional, energetic); trauma-informed crisis leadership; the needs beneath disappointment, anger, fear, grief and relief.
5. Leading from energetic safety: heart-brain coherence, equanimity, the heart breathing technique; "the most regulated person in the room is the most powerful person in the room".
6. Strategic storytelling: crisis as an onion, layered communication, the long-term view; the BBC's WhatsApp service during the Ebola outbreak.
7. From tragedy to trust: the Maple Leaf Foods 2008 listeria case study and CEO Michael McCain's response; brings the first six months together.

Planned topics for months 8 to 12 (the course page says topics can change and some modules may be substituted): 8 timelines of response and owning your narrative; 9 crisis as a leadership mirror; 10 owning accountability without fuelling liability (the art of the public apology); 11 post-crisis, from surviving to strengthening; 12 becoming the Crisis Ready® communicator. If someone wants the detail on months 8 to 12, offer to pass the question to the team.
