# Traffic forecast: Melissa Agnes pages and articles

Prepared 10 September 2026 by 3P Digital. A forecast, not a target. Push on the assumptions before quoting the numbers.

## Baseline (Google Search Console and GA4, from the 2 September review)

| Measure | June to August 2026 |
|---|---|
| Australian search impressions | 2,148 (about 300 to 450 a week) |
| Australian search clicks | 19 (about 6 a month, almost all brand searches) |
| Worldwide search clicks | 24 |
| Organic search sessions (GA4) | 115 for the quarter, 26 in August |
| Pages indexed | 16, rising to 19 on 27 August and 35 with this release |
| Average position | 37.6 (deep testing on non-brand queries) |

Every attributable click landed on the homepage. No page other than the homepage had earned a search click in three months.

## Method

Per page: an estimated monthly Australian search volume for the target cluster, an expected ranking band by month for a young domain with internal links and clean structure, the click-through rate that band earns, and a long-tail multiplier of about 1.5 for the queries Google matches that are not the head phrase. Worldwide traffic is counted at a discount because it rarely converts to an Australian enrolment.

Ranking assumptions for a page on this domain, with no new backlinks:

| Age of page | Typical position band | Share of impressions clicked |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 to 2 | 20 to 50 (indexing and testing) | under 1% |
| Month 3 | 12 to 30 | 1 to 2% |
| Month 6 | 5 to 15 | 3 to 6% |
| Month 12 | 3 to 10 on the long tail, 8 to 20 on head terms | 5 to 10% |

## What moves the number

- A link from crisisreadyinstitute.com or Melissa's own profile to /melissa-agnes. The single biggest lever: it hands the page authority for her name and lifts every page linked from it.
- Adding the formula, self-check and presenter pages to the site navigation. The site nav is unchanged; the new pages receive links only from articles, the trainers page and each other.
- Publishing cadence. One article a fortnight from Melissa's newsletters keeps the topic cluster growing and keeps Google recrawling.
- The LinkedIn quote cards and the fortnightly emails. Their traffic shows as social and email, not organic, but the engagement signals help the pages rank.
- Search Console sitemap resubmission and IndexNow on the day of deploy.

## What the traffic is worth

At the base case, month 12 brings about 800 sessions a month to pages that all route into the Crisis Masterclass. If one in a hundred of those visitors reaches the enrolment page and one in ten of those enrols, that is roughly one enrolment a month from search, or about $4,080 a year each at $340 a month. The conservative case is one every two months; the strong case two a month. These conversion rates are assumptions to be replaced with GA4 data once the pages have a quarter behind them.
