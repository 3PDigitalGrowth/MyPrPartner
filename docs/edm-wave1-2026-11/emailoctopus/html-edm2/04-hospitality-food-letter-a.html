A few weeks ago I wrote to you about how a one-star pile-on can start with a fake photo.

I said the standard line has stopped working: "We are aware of the allegations and are investigating."

Here's a better first sentence:

"We know this news is concerning, and if you're feeling worried or frustrated right now, we completely understand."

It works because of a rule Melissa Agnes teaches in month three of the Crisis Masterclass: you can't beat emotion with logic. People who are frightened or angry can't take in your facts yet, so the order matters.

Validate. Name what people are feeling, and let them feel it.
Relate. Connect it to something you both care about.
Prove. Then give the facts, and what you're doing about them.

Most statements start at step three. A plan can't fix that. Practice does.
