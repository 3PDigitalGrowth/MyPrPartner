Lyall Mercer of CRC Public Relations shares a message for P World Crisis Comms Bootcamp delegates, plus an exclusive 14-day trial of the Crisis Masterclass.

To my fellow P World Crisis Comms Bootcamp delegates

What an awesome time in Sydney a couple of weeks ago at the bootcamp. Thanks to Kosta, Ilhan and the P World team for bringing it to Australia, and to the speakers who made the trek across the oceans!

It was an honour to be involved and support the bootcamp, and meet so many passionate communications professionals.

In his wrap up, Kosta mentioned that he'd be sending you something from me, so here it is.

One of the things I enjoyed most about the bootcamp was the authenticity. No one pretended to have all the answers, and we all walked away with something new to think about.

A common theme was how much our profession continues to evolve. While the foundation of trust remains the same, AI, misinformation, and stakeholder expectations are changing the way we must operate.

That's why I believe continual learning is no longer optional. It's part of the responsibility we have to the organisations and communities we serve.

Our company partnered with P World because they are raising the standard of crisis leadership.

It's for this same reason that we have recently partnered with Melissa Agnes, CEO of the Crisis Ready Institute in the USA, to bring her Crisis Ready methodology to Australia through our new 12-month online (do at your own pace) crisis masterclass.

You may already know of Melissa, as she has advised organisations around the world including NATO and ministries of foreign affairs and defence on crisis readiness and leadership, and brings a different perspective to traditional crisis communications training.

Ultimately, organisations are only as prepared as the people leading them: those who create the crisis strategies and develop the messaging, and the spokespeople who stand in front of their stakeholders and ask for their trust at the most difficult times.

As someone who's heard many excellent crisis speakers, what stood out to me while I was working through the program with Melissa was her emphasis on understanding the emotions driving stakeholder behaviour during a crisis. It's challenged my thinking and genuinely strengthened the way I approach crisis communications.
